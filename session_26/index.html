World Science Day

World Science Day for Peace and Development is an international event celebrated annually on 10 November. Established by UNESCO in 2001, the day aims to highlight the critical role that science plays in society and to engage the wider public in discussions regarding emerging scientific issues. By bridging the gap between science and everyday life, it underscores how scientific advancements contribute to building peaceful, sustainable communities around the globe.

Scientists

Scientists are researchers and experts who systematically gather knowledge to understand how the physical and natural world operates. Using the scientific method, they formulate hypotheses, conduct rigorous experiments, and analyze data to uncover objective truths.
